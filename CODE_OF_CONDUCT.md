# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MouseKy a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing
private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security-reporting
channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban offenders.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
